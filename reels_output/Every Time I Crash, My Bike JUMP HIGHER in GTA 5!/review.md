# Export review

- Four distinct moments were selected after reviewing the source contact sheets and tighter samples around the stunt sequences.
- Each 16:9 source clip was center-cropped to 9:16 so the rider and bike fill the reel frame; the subject stays near the center in the sampled start, middle and end frames.
- All four MP4s passed FFmpeg decode checks at 1080×1920 and 30 fps.
- English translated subtitles were added from Hindi commentary. Karaoke word highlights use aligned words when available; complete static phrases are used when the word aligner omits tokens.
- Caption position, readability and sample word highlighting were checked in rendered frames. Full listening review was not performed, so machine translation may contain occasional recognition/translation errors.
