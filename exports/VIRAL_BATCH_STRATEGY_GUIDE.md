## 1. Overview & Formats Architecture

Short-form platforms (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok) prioritize three core signals:
1. **Audience Retention Rate (AVD > 85%)**: Viewers must stay through the 30-35 second duration.
2. **First 2-Second Hook Velocity**: Immediate text overlay and high-contrast visuals stopping the infinite scroll.
3. **Loop & Comment Engagement Ratio**: Questions, tier rankings, and contentious comparisons prompting comment debates.

To maximize viral reach beyond simple raw stream clipping, content is structured into **10 distinct content archetypes** styled with the **Hormozi subtitle standard** (Yellow `#FFFF00` primary text, dynamic word-pop bounce animations, contextual sentiment emojis, and top hook cards).

## 4. Stream Slicing & Chat Spike Analysis Protocol

Instead of downloading 20GB-50GB raw stream VODs, our production pipeline executes a surgical 3-stage protocol:

```
[1. Chat Mining]
TwitchDownloaderCLI chatdownload --id <VOD_ID> -o chat.json
        │
        ▼
[2. Spike Detection]
reels.py find-chat-spikes --chat chat.json --top 10 --bucket 20 --window 5.0
        │
        ▼
[3. Surgical VOD Slicing]
TwitchDownloaderCLI videodownload --id <VOD_ID> -b <Start>s -e <End>s -o slice.mp4
```

### Key Parameters:
- `--bucket 20`: Sliding 20-second calculation bucket for reaction density.
- `--window 5.0`: 5-minute rolling baseline to filter out natural slow chat hours.
- `--gap 120`: Minimum 2-minute gap between clips to prevent clip overlapping.
- `--skip-intro 15`: Ignores the stream's opening "starting soon" countdown screen.

## 5. Hormozi Caption Engine & Visual Formatting

### Subtitle Specifications (`hormozi` preset):
- **Font**: Arial Black / Montserrat ExtraBold (All-Caps).
- **Primary Color**: Vivid Yellow (`&H0000FFFF` / `#FFFF00`).
- **Highlight Accent**: Pure White (`&H00FFFFFF` / `#FFFFFF`).
- **Outline & Shadow**: 5px solid black outline (`&H00000000`), 4px deep shadow.
- **Animation**: Dynamic word-pop bounce tags (`{\t(0,80,\fscx120\fscy120)\t(80,180,\fscx100\fscy100)}`).
- **Pacing**: 2 to 3 words max per subtitle burst for rapid ocular engagement.
- **Hook Position**: Pinned to the upper safe zone (`alignment=8`, 250px margin).
- **Audio Standards**: EBU R128 `loudnorm` target: integrated loudness `-16 LUFS`, true peak `-1.5 dBTP`.
