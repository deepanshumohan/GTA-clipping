2. **Alex Hormozi Caption Typography**:
   - **Burst Length**: 1–3 words per screen, timed with millisecond precision to natural speech.
   - **Font**: Heavy, thick all-caps sans-serif (`Arial BoldMT`) with **6px solid black outline** and **4px drop shadow** (100% opaque) for mobile readability.
   - **Color Palette**: Vivid Yellow base text (`#FFFF00`) with Neon Green (`#00FF00`) keyword highlights on verbs, numbers, and takeaways.
   - **Bright Red Punctuation**: All exclamation marks (`!`) and question marks (`?`) styled in high-visibility bright red (`#FF0000`).
   - **Zero Emoji Rectangles**: All unicode emoji symbols stripped to prevent missing glyph rectangle (`□`) rendering bugs.
   - **Dynamic Motion**: Pop/scale-bounce animation (`{\fscx112\fscy112\t(0,80,\fscx100\fscy100)}`) on each card burst.
   - **Safe-Zone Placement**: Captions placed in the lower-middle zone (`MarginV: 520`), clear of TikTok/Reels UI buttons and facecam areas.

3. **Natural Variable Durations**:
   - Durations naturally vary between **24.2s and 34.0s** based on the scene's organic climax and punchline.
