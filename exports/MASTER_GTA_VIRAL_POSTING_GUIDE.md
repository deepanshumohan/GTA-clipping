## 1. Production & Styling Compliance Summary

- **Microsoft Edge TTS Voiceover Engine (`edge-tts`)**:
  - High-clarity neural voice (`en-US-ChristopherNeural`).
  - **No Monologue Throughout**: Voiceover is strictly used for the **first 2.5–5.0 seconds** to set up the context and hook (e.g. "Can you finally stop the train in GTA 6? Watch this!").
  - **Raw Gameplay Dominance**: Immediately after the hook intro, the voiceover stops completely. The creator's live screams, police sirens, screeching tires, metal destruction, and game audio ramp to 100% full volume for the remainder of the video!
- **Zero Missing Glyphs (Tofu Boxes `□`)**:
  - 100% verified. All emojis and decorative non-ASCII characters stripped so subtitle rendering in `Arial Bold` is crisp and completely free of `□` boxes.
- **Bright Red Punctuation**:
  - Every `!` and `?` formatted in bright red (`{\c&H000000FF&}`).
- **Alex Hormozi Typography**:
  - Heavy all-caps sans-serif (`Arial BoldMT`), 6px black outline, 4px shadow, 1–3 words/burst, neon green (`#00FF00`) accents on keywords and numbers, vivid yellow (`#FFFF00`) base text, and dynamic word-pop bounce animation.
- **100% GTA 6 Context**:
  - Zero GTA 5 footage (GamerFleet removed, GTA 5 map removed, non-GTA games removed).
  - Authentic live-stream gameplay used for train stopping, 5-star police chases, Vice City driving, and in-game TikTok doomscrolling.
